In product copy, never refer to a human agent by name ("Anita") or pronoun ("she") before that person has actually joined the conversation and introduced themselves. Until then use formal terms: "relationship manager", "support executive", "human agent". System/assistant copy must also never say "myself" or speak in confusing first person.

**Why:** Tejas, on the [[jupiter-emi-project]] flows (Aug 2026): "I don't know who Anita is... We should never refer to someone as Anita until Anita has stated that Anita has joined the chat." Vague or personified copy reads as confusing, not warm.

**How to apply:** Sweep all screens, dialogs, and dock states for premature names/pronouns. Pairs with [[fintech-voice]]-style rules: direct, objective, verb-first, exact numbers, no vague reassurance lines.
