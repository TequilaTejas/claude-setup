# Fintech voice

Grounded in how shipped money apps write (Wise, Revolut, Ubank, Afterpay, Monzo — via Mobbin): the calmer the money moment, the shorter the sentence.

## Voice

Money is stressful; the product isn't. The app talks like a competent friend who happens to be a bank: direct, warm, unhurried, never clever at the user's expense.

## Rules

1. **Verb-first actions.** Buttons start with the verb and name the object: "Confirm and send", "Pay ₹4,500", "Allow access". Never "Submit", "OK", "Proceed".
2. **Outcome-first confirmations.** Success copy states what is now true, in two or three words if possible: "Reminder sent." "Transfer scheduled." "Paused." Detail comes after, never before.
3. **Exact numbers, always.** "₹300 late fee", "0 fees (included)", "26 more days". A vague quantity ("a small fee", "soon") reads as hiding something.
4. **Positive instruction over negative warning.** Lead with the action that avoids the bad thing: "Pay in 3 days to avoid the ₹300 late fee" beats "A late fee will be applied if you don't pay".
5. **Second person, contractions.** "You're up to date." "I'll check in 2 days before every debit." Formal third-person banking voice is forbidden.
6. **Facts fit one line.** If a fact needs two lines, it's two facts or too wordy.
7. **State what's included and what's not.** Fees, interest, credit impact — say the cost or say it's zero. "Free" only when literally ₹0.
8. **No drama words.** Banned: penalty (prefer the exact fee), warning, failure, urgent, immediately, unfortunately, "we regret". Urgency comes from a date and a number, not an adjective.
9. **Reassure with mechanism, not adjectives.** "Looking these up leaves no mark on your credit score" beats "completely safe and secure".
10. **The assistant speaks as I, the institution as we.** "I checked your cycle" (assistant), "We can review again later" (policy). Never mix in one sentence.

## Tone dial

- Routine moments (balances, receipts): neutral, near-silent. Two-word confirmations.
- Stressful moments (overdue, declines): warmest register, shortest sentences, always end with the way forward.
- Celebration: quiet. A period, not an exclamation mark. The number is the celebration.

## House constraints (this project)

No exclamation marks, no emoji, no em dashes. Relative time for spans ("in 3 days"), absolute dates only at payment anchors. Never "they" for a named agent. Never shame the user.
